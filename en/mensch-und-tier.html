Work area under development

Humans & Animals: healing each other

People and animals who have experienced war, displacement, loss or violence can help each other.

War leaves wounds you cannot see – in people and in animals. Children who have lost everything. Adults who can no longer sleep. Cats and dogs that come out of the rubble frightened and trust no one any more.

We are convinced that the two can give each other support: a child who looks after a rescued cat takes on responsibility and experiences closeness. An animal that is cared for can learn to trust again. This is exactly where this work area comes in – abroad as well as in Germany.

What we would like to build

Animal-assisted activities

Encounters with animals that help people process experiences of war and loss – always under professional guidance and with consideration for the animals' welfare.

Psychosocial support

Support and stabilisation for people who are emotionally burdened by war, displacement or loss, and referral to professional help.

Care for frightened animals

Taking in, caring for and rehabilitating animals that have been injured or frightened by war, neglect or abuse.

Sharing knowledge

Education about the emotional consequences of war and displacement, about the bond between humans and animals, and about treating animals responsibly.

In Gaza and in Germany

In Gaza

We would like children and families to be able to help at our animal welfare project and, in doing so, experience care, responsibility and community – in the middle of surroundings full of loss.

In Germany

For refugees, and for people who have relatives in war zones or have lost them, we would like to create animal-assisted and supportive activities together with professionals and partner organisations.

This area is under development. Activities will only be created with qualified professionals and with animal welfare in mind.
