“We want to create an evening that does not end with the final applause – but moves people to share responsibility.”

One evening is not enough. A beginning can change a lot.

Humanitarian work needs more than attention for a single moment. It needs people who look closely, build relationships and turn opportunities into concrete support.

The planned International Charity Gala in Frankfurt is meant to make the mission of Dar Al-Rahma something people can experience: not as an ordinary reception, but as a meeting of culture, social engagement and a humanitarian mission that people can follow and understand.

Our conviction

People in crisis regions are not only recipients of aid. They have voices, memories, skills, stories and hopes for the future. This dignity should be visible in how we present our projects.

Why Frankfurt?

As an international city, Frankfurt is a good place to bring together people from business, culture, civil society and humanitarian engagement – as a starting point for connections that last beyond the evening.

“We do not want an event about the suffering of others. We want an event that respects their dignity and creates room for concrete solidarity.”

How the gala should feel

Not separate items on a programme, but one connected story.

  1. 01

    Arrive & meet

    A stylish reception in a respectful, international setting – with first cultural and humanitarian insights.

  2. 02

    Experience culture

    Palestinian art, music, poetry and other contributions put identity and creativity at the centre – curated rather than reduced to folklore.

  3. 03

    Understand stories

    Vetted project presentations, documentary contributions and voices from the field create closeness without reducing people to their plight.

  4. 04

    Share responsibility

    A clearly explained need for aid and clear ways to take part show how attention becomes support.

  5. 05

    Take something with you

    Conversation starters, new partnerships and the awareness that the real work continues afterwards.

These elements describe the intended dramaturgy. Performances, guests, times, venue and programme are in planning and not firmly confirmed.

Palestine should not just be a topic. Palestine should have a voice.

Culture carries memory, belonging and the future. That is why the gala should not reduce Palestinian identity to images of crisis: art, literature, music, traditional crafts and contemporary perspectives show how diverse this culture is.

The Humanitarian Impact Gallery

A planned exhibition area invites visitors to linger and understand, independently of the stage programme: curated project stations, documentary photographs, personal stories and explanations of aid needs.

Cultural imagery

Tatreez embroidery, calligraphy or Palestinian music – conveyed by cultural practitioners and experts who explain the background authentically. Aesthetics and content should support each other, not replace one another.

What we will not do: no sensationalist portrayals, no exploitation of personal fates, no unverified claims, no publication of sensitive information about children, families or contributors in Gaza. Usage rights, consent and safety will be checked for all materials.

Eleven areas of work – many ways to contribute

From event production through organisation and communication to culture and humanitarian project work. Tasks are assigned according to need and by mutual agreement.

  1. 01

    Project coordination & staffing

    Build structures, coordinate tasks and schedules, support teamwork.

    Organisational talent and an eye for reliable processes

  2. 02

    Venue & event production

    Coordinate venues, equipment, logistics, service providers and technology.

    Experience in events, technology, production or operational planning

  3. 03

    Sponsoring & fundraising

    Approach funding partners, prepare documents, support reputable cooperations.

    Communication skills, structure, a sense for responsible partnerships

  4. 04

    Finance & legal

    Provide professional support for calculations, documentation, contracts and compliant processes.

    Business, accounting or legal knowledge

  5. 05

    Ticketing & guest management

    Help shape invitations, guest communication, registration, reception and hosting.

    Enjoyment of service, precision and discreet hospitality

  6. 06

    VIP & Celebrity Relations

    Professionally support coordination with possible guests of honour, artists or their representatives.

    Experience in guest care, protocol or confidential communication

  7. 07

    Programme & culture

    Develop contributions, moderation, artistic programme items and a coherent dramaturgy.

    For people from culture, stage, editorial work and creative planning

  8. 08

    Humanitarian content & Gaza projects

    Put project information in context, check facts, prepare a dignified presentation for the stage and the gallery.

    Care, research skills, sensitivity towards vulnerable people

  9. 09

    Marketing, PR & media

    Communication strategy, design, social media, press work and media support.

    Experience in writing, design, photography, video or media

  10. 10

    Palestine Team

    Support cultural and linguistic context and advise on sensitive portrayals.

    Language, cultural and community expertise

  11. 11

    Ethics & partner review

    Carefully review proposed cooperations, partners and service providers – according to shared criteria such as integrity, transparency, respect for human rights and legal soundness.

    An analytical eye, good documentation, fair and objective assessment

Naming an area does not mean that a fixed position or task has already been promised there. We will talk about involvement, scope and conditions individually.
