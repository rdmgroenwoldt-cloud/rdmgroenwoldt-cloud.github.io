Compassion · Care · Hope

Our own project: animal welfare in the middle of North Gaza, in the Yellow Zone. We rescue, feed and care for cats and dogs – where hardly anyone goes any more.

Our place

We have a plot of land with a house. The house is partly destroyed – yet it has become a home for our animals.

Cat area

We built a dedicated area for the cats, where they live protected and are looked after.

Quarantine area

Newly rescued and sick animals go into quarantine first, so that diseases don't spread.

Winterproofing

Winter is here. We are trying to secure the damaged house against rain, wind and cold as well as we can – for this we urgently need help.

What we plan next

Animal welfare hand in hand with humanitarian aid: doctors for humans also patch up animals, vets also help people.

Feeding stations

Fixed places where street animals regularly find food and water.

Neutering projects

For dogs and cats – the most effective means against new animal suffering on the street.

Vet network

Involve more vets on the ground – and specialists abroad who advise by video.

Basic medicine stock

A fixed supply of medicines so that in an emergency we don't have to start searching first.

Care

Daily care, follow-up treatment and nursing weakened animals back to health.

Collaboration

With medical relief projects on the ground and with associations outside Gaza. Learn more

Vaccination & deworming

Vaccination and deworming programmes – to protect animals and people, for example against rabies.

Rehoming

Placing healthy animals in good hands; animals that nobody takes stay with us for good.

Training & education

Training helpers and raising awareness about treating animals responsibly.
