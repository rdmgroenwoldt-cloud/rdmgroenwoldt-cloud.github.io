We are founding a non-profit UG

The help already exists. Now it gets a permanent umbrella.

Where we stand today

Our projects already exist and help every day: Dar Al-Rahma Cat Rescue rescues and cares for cats and dogs in North Gaza, and Kids4Hope accompanies 140 orphans as they learn. So far they are carried by dedicated private individuals – with their own effort, their own money and the support of friends and donors.

Important: The non-profit UG (gUG) has not yet been founded. It therefore does not yet accept donations and cannot yet issue donation receipts. Until then, you can support the projects directly.

Why we are founding a gUG

What began as private help has grown. To keep it reliable and lasting, it needs a firm structure.

Reliability

The work should not depend on individuals, but stand on a firm, legally ordered foundation.

Transparency

Clear rules for donations, receipts, review and reports – set down in articles of association.

Donation receipts

Once the tax office recognises non-profit status, donations can be taken into account for tax purposes.

One umbrella for many projects

Our own projects and partner projects under one roof – in Gaza today, elsewhere later.

Making more possible

Culture, inclusion, integration and events such as the planned Gala 2027 need a reliable organisation behind them.

Winning partners

Associations, foundations and companies find it easier to work with a recognised non-profit organisation.

The path to the gUG

  1. 1

    Draft the articles of association

    Define purposes, rules and oversight – the draft is ready.

  2. 2

    Legal review

    A lawyer reviews the articles; the tax office is asked for an assessment in advance.

  3. 3

    Founding at the notary

    Notarisation, appointment of the management, payment of the share capital.

  4. 4

    Entry in the commercial register

    Only then does the gUG exist legally.

  5. 5

    Recognition of non-profit status

    The tax office reviews the articles. Only after that may donation receipts be issued.

  6. 6

    The projects move under the umbrella

    Donations will then run through the gUG. We will keep you informed here and under News.

Current status: step 1. We cannot yet name a date for the founding.
