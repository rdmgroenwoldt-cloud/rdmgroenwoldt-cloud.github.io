Privacy policy

This translation is provided for information only. The German version is legally binding.

In short: This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking, no analytics or advertising services, no embedded social media content and no contact form. Fonts, images and videos are hosted on our own web space. What data is nevertheless generated is set out here.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, German: DSGVO) is the person named in the Legal notice:
Max Mustermann, Musterstraße 1, 12345 Musterstadt, email: info@example.org.
After the non-profit UG is founded, it will take their place.

2. Hosting: GitHub Pages

This website is delivered via GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data so that the page can be displayed – in particular your device's IP address, date and time, the address requested, and information about your browser and operating system (server log data). This data also serves the security of the service.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub belongs to Microsoft; we base the transfer on the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). More on this in GitHub's privacy statement.

[RECHTSPRÜFUNG: aktuelle Zertifizierung von GitHub/Microsoft unter dem Data Privacy Framework und Speicherdauer der Logdaten vor der endgültigen Veröffentlichung prüfen. Bei einem Umzug auf einen eigenen Server in der EU wird dieser Abschnitt ersetzt.]

3. Fonts

The fonts “Cormorant Garamond” and “Inter” are hosted on our own web space. When a page is opened, no connection is made to Google or other font providers.

4. Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. We store three small entries in your browser's local storage (localStorage). They stay on your device and are not transmitted to us or to third parties. An overview is on the page Cookies & local storage.

Because these entries serve only to provide a feature you use (access to this website, which is not yet public; closing the cookie notice; and your language choice in the wish calendar), no consent is needed for them (Section 25 (2) No. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete the entries at any time – with one click on the cookie page or in your browser settings.

5. Links to other providers

We link to other websites, for example to sources (UN, WHO, media), to the projects' own pages, to Instagram, TikTok, GoFundMe and kids4hope.com. As long as you don't click a link, no data is transmitted to these providers. Only when you follow it do their own privacy provisions apply there.

6. Donations and sponsorships

No payments are processed through this website and no donor data is collected. Those who donate do so directly with the respective project or provider (for example PayPal or GoFundMe); their privacy provisions apply there. Your selection on the page Cat sponsorships stays in your browser; “Copy selection” only places it in your own clipboard.

7. Contact by email

If you write us an email, we process your details (email address, name, content) in order to answer your enquiry – for enquiries about sponsorship or getting involved under Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR, otherwise under Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR. We delete the message once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations stand in the way.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just write us an email for this.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority where you live or the data protection authority of the federal state in which we have our seat.
