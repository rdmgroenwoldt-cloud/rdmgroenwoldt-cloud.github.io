Work area under development

Inclusion & Integration

No one should be left out – not because of their origin, language, faith or a disability.

Compassion doesn't begin only in the crisis zone. It begins where we live: in the neighbourhood, in the club, at school, at work. With inclusion and integration, we want to help people here in Germany take part on equal terms – and turn strangers into neighbours.

What matters to us

Encounters as equals

Integration is not a one-way street. We create places where people learn from one another.

Participation for everyone

Our activities should be open to people with and without disabilities – as barrier-free as possible.

Volunteering as a bridge

Those who help belong. Volunteering connects people of different backgrounds.

Planned activities

  1. 1

    Meet-ups

    Open meetings for newcomers and long-time residents – with conversation, food and culture.

  2. 2

    Learning and language sponsorships

    Volunteers accompany children, young people and adults as they learn and practise speaking German.

  3. 3

    Workshops & orientation

    Help with everyday life: dealing with authorities, education, training – passed on by people who have walked these paths themselves.

  4. 4

    Helping together

    People of different backgrounds organise campaigns for our projects together – and get to know each other in the process.

  5. 5

    Support in hard times

    Counselling and psychosocial support for refugees and for people who have relatives in war and crisis zones or have lost them – also with animal-assisted activities (Humans & Animals).

  6. 6

    Inclusion of people with disabilities

    Activities designed so that everyone can take part.

The activities are in planning. Anyone who would like to get involved is warmly welcome.

Inclusion and integration are closely linked to Culture & identity: those who feel their own background is valued can meet others more openly.
