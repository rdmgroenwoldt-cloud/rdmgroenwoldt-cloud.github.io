Mission & Cycle

For us, help is not a one-way street. It is a cycle.

The cycle of togetherness

Between home and abroad

Help flows from Germany to Gaza: food, medicines, lessons, a roof for the winter. And something flows back from Gaza: stories, culture, courage and the knowledge of people who carry on under the hardest conditions. Here in Germany, we turn that into encounters – with culture evenings, education and integration. That is how help becomes an exchange.

Between humans and animals

People rescue animals from the rubble. And animals help people: those who have experienced war, displacement and loss often find calm, trust and support in contact with an animal. Animal welfare also protects people – for example from rabies. In Gaza, doctors for humans also patch up animals, and vets also help people. For us, humans and animals belong together. Learn more

Between giving and receiving

Those who give also receive: community, meaning and the knowledge of being needed. And those who receive help give something back – trust, experience, culture. With us there is no above and below. There is a family in which everyone contributes something.

What everything rests on

Togetherness

We work with the people on the ground, not over their heads. We make decisions together.

Morals & ethics

We act honestly, responsibly and traceably – towards the people and animals we help, and towards everyone who trusts us.

Humanity

Every life counts. Dignity cannot be divided – not by origin, religion or species.

Our principles

  • Humanity: We help where there is need – people and animals.
  • Impartiality: Help is based on need alone, not on origin, religion or conviction.
  • Neutrality and independence: We are independent of political parties and pursue no party-political goals.
  • Transparency: We show what happens with every bit of help. Learn more
  • Protection: The wellbeing and safety of children, families and helpers come before any interest in information.
What we stand for: respectful coexistence of people of every origin and religion. We oppose every form of hostility towards people – in particular antisemitism, anti-Muslim racism and racism.
