Medical Point North Gaza

In an emergency shelter, Ibrahim and Lamees stabilise the injured until the ambulance arrives – wounds, burns, chronically ill people. With hardly any supplies and great skill.

More about the Medical PointHow it all began, what is needed, video

Ibrahim, 22 years old and training to become a nurse, first treated the injured in front of his own tent. In an emergency shelter, Lamees turned this into a permanent point of contact. What is needed: emergency, bandaging and suturing supplies as well as medicines – antibiotics, painkillers, burn ointments and medicines for chronically ill people.

Education · Gaza

Kids4Hope

Hasan and his team accompany 140 orphaned children in their learning – in their own school tent, with notebooks, colours and people who are reliably there for them.

  • 140 orphaned children
  • 1 school tent of their own
  • 93% of schools in Gaza damaged
kids4hope.com ↗ Support
More about Kids4HopeWhat is needed, video

Lessons, reading and writing, crafts and painting, and small celebrations such as the certificate presentation – a touch of normality in the midst of hardship. What is needed: notebooks, pens and books, tables, benches and tarpaulins, small meals as well as clothing and blankets for the winter. For us, Kids4Hope belongs to the umbrella area Children's Relief.

Water & family aid · Gaza

Hope4Humanity & Gazahilfspool

Drinking water, wells and hot meals – and, with the Gazahilfspool, regular sponsorships for twelve families. A private initiative; the association hope4humanity e.V. is being founded.

  • 15,000 litres of drinking water
  • 12 sponsored families
  • Wells: €5,500 and €3,400 needed
