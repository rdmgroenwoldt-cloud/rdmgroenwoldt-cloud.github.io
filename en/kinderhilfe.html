Kids4Hope

Education project in Gaza

Hasan and his team accompany 140 orphans as they learn. They give the children room to ask questions, discover new things and enjoy their progress.

Kids4Hope is an independent project, run by Hasan and his team – accompanied and supported by Dar Al-Rahma. To the project page with photos and video → · www.kids4hope.com ↗
