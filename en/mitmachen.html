Collaboration

Our projects are meant to interlock – and we like to work with others. This is how we imagine it:

Non-profit partners

Associations and organisations – from medical aid organisations and education initiatives to animal welfare societies – can work with us to fund projects, run fundraising campaigns or organise relief supplies. They receive reports and receipts from us.

Practice and clinic partnerships

Medical practices, clinics or vet practices take on a remote sponsorship: advice by video, help with medicine lists and treatment plans.

Help in crisis zones

We seek exchange with organisations that have experience in emergency medical aid, education and animal welfare in crisis zones.

Companies & retailers

Companies can take on a sponsorship, start a fundraising campaign or donate part of their revenue.

Schools, communities, associations

Sponsored runs, project days or bazaars – we provide photos, stories and material for them.

Are you an association, a practice or a project and would like to work with us? We'd be delighted – contact details to follow.
