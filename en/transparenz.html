Transparency

Trust grows when you can see what happens with your help.

The path of a donation

  1. 1

    Your donation

    You donate through one of our ways to donate – earmarked, if you wish, for animal welfare or children's aid.

  2. 2

    Arrival at the project

    Until the gUG is founded, your support goes directly to the private individuals who run the project; afterwards, to the gUG.

  3. 3

    Planning with the team on the ground

    Together with the people on the ground, we decide what is needed most urgently.

  4. 4

    Approval under the two-person rule

    Expenses are reviewed and approved by at least two people.

  5. 5

    Implementation and receipts

    Food, medicines, treatments or lessons are paid for; receipts and photos document how the money was used.

  6. 6

    Report to you

    We report under News, in our channels and, for our sponsors, personally.

Our principles

Earmarking

We use earmarked donations only for the stated purpose.

Receipts

Every expense is documented – as far as the situation on the ground allows.

Vetting partners

We carefully vet partners, recipients and service providers before we work together – also in line with legal requirements.

Honesty

We clearly separate work done, current needs and plans. No unchecked figures.

Protection

We do not publish any information that could endanger children, families or helpers in Gaza.

On the way to the gUG

With the planned non-profit UG, these principles will be set down in binding form in articles of association – funds then only for non-profit purposes, no distribution of profits.

What we publish

  • Reports on our rescues and projects
  • An annual overview of income and expenses, once the first financial year has ended
  • The status of the founding of the gUG – and, after the founding, registered office, register and non-profit status

Until the gUG is founded, there are no donation receipts. Support goes directly to the projects as a private gift.
