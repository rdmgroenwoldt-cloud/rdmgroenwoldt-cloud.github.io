Our mission

Compassion should not stop at a border – not between countries, not between humans and animals.

Dar Al-Rahma – the “House of Compassion” – wants to help people and animals in need effectively, with dignity and in a traceable way. We bring humanitarian aid, animal welfare, education and culture together under one roof. We listen to the people on the ground, support their own initiatives and make visible what happens with every bit of help.

At the centre is a cycle: between home and abroad, between humans and animals, between giving and receiving – carried by togetherness, morals, ethics and humanity. To Mission & Cycle

What we want to achieve

Our goals, step by step. How far we get depends on the support we receive.

Now – the coming months

  1. 1

    Getting through the winter

    Secure the partly destroyed house of our animal welfare project against rain and cold as well as possible.

  2. 2

    Securing basic care

    Food, care and veterinary treatment for all animals in our care – and a first basic stock of medicines.

  3. 3

    Securing Kids4Hope

    Support Hasan and his team in continuing to accompany the 140 orphans as they learn – and, with the founding, bring Kids4Hope under the umbrella of the gUG as a project in its own right.

  4. 4

    Founding the non-profit UG

    A permanent umbrella with articles of association and clear processes for donations, receipts and reports, so that every bit of help stays traceable. Status of the founding

Medium term – by 2027

  1. 5

    Neutering and feeding stations

    Launch neutering projects for dogs and cats and set up fixed feeding stations for street animals.

  2. 6

    Vet network

    Involve more vets on the ground and win specialists abroad for advice.

  3. 7

    Culture and encounters

    First culture evenings, workshops and meeting formats in Germany.

  4. 8

    International Charity Gala 2027

    An evening in Frankfurt that combines culture, encounters and support – and reaches far beyond.

Long term

  1. 9

    Growing where help is needed

    As the Dar Al-Rahma family, help people and animals in need step by step in other parts of the world too – always together with the people on the ground.

  2. 10

    Strengthening partners

    Support more projects of people on the ground who are already doing good work – and connect them with one another.

These are goals, not promises. We report on progress under News.
