Who we are

House of Compassion

Assalamu alaikum

“Dar Al-Rahma” means “House of Compassion”. The Dar Al-Rahma Center is an umbrella: our own projects run under it – and projects of other people on the ground who are already doing valuable work also find support there.

The Center brings together people who contribute their time, their experience and their ideas to help others. For us, it has become a family. We rejoice together, stand by each other on difficult days and share responsibility.

Our work began in Gaza – with rescuing cats in North Gaza and supporting children. Today we combine humanitarian aid, animal welfare, education, culture and encounters.

One umbrella, two ways to help

Our focus is North Gaza.

Supported projects

What we stand for

Dignity

People in crisis zones are not just recipients of aid. They have voices, abilities, stories and hopes for the future – and we show that.

Hand in hand

Animal welfare and humanitarian aid belong together. In Gaza, doctors for humans also patch up animals – and vets also help people.

Listening on the ground

We don't bring ready-made solutions. Together with the people on the ground, we decide what is needed most urgently.

One family

We stand together – on good days and hard ones – and share responsibility.

Transparency

We distinguish between work done, current needs and plans – and show what happens with help.

Protection

We do not publish sensitive information about children, families or helpers in Gaza.
