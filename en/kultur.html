Work area under development

Culture & Identity

Palestine should not just be a topic. Palestine should have a voice.

Culture carries memory, belonging and future. Especially when houses, schools and archives are destroyed, culture becomes what remains and is passed on. That is why cultural exchange and identity work are part of humanitarian work for us.

We do not want to reduce Palestinian identity to images of crisis. Art, literature, music, traditional crafts and contemporary perspectives show how diverse this culture is – curated rather than shortened into folklore.

What we would like to preserve and show

Tatreez

The traditional Palestinian art of embroidery: every pattern tells of a region, a village, a family.

Calligraphy

Script as art – between tradition and contemporary design.

Music & poetry

Songs, poems and stories that keep memory alive.

Cuisine & hospitality

Dishes that connect families and generations – and welcome guests.

Contemporary art

Young artists with their own perspectives on origin, present and future.

Stories

Personal accounts that show people as people – with dignity and with their consent.

Identity work

For children and young people growing up in Gaza or far from their roots, the question “Who am I, where do I come from?” is especially important. For us, identity work means creating spaces where origin, language and culture are valued – and where young people find their own voice.

Identity is not something to set against others. It is a foundation for meeting others openly. That is why identity work and Inclusion & integration belong together for us.

Remembering, understanding, passing on

Commemoration & remembrance

Exhibitions and documentation that remember the victims of war and disasters – with dignity and without sensational images.

Language & history

Children and young people of Palestinian origin should know their language, history and culture – and at the same time be a natural part of society here.

Education evenings

Talks and discussions on history and the present, human rights and international humanitarian law – with different perspectives and verifiable sources.
