The restricted zone

The “Yellow Line”

Since the ceasefire in October 2025, the so-called “Yellow Line” has separated the areas under Israeli control from the rest of the Gaza Strip. According to the UN, the areas with access restrictions cover around 65 percent of the Strip (as of 26 Jun 2026). That leaves the population with about 35 percent of the land – roughly 128 km² for 2.1 million people.

around 65% with access restrictions
approx. 35%

OCHA, 26 Jun 2026 · Al Jazeera, 12 Oct 2026. Depending on the source and the method of measurement, the figures vary between 53 and 70 percent.

What is happening at the line right nowAs of November 2026

On 18 and 19 November 2026, Israeli ground troops advanced beyond the Yellow Line for the first time since the ceasefire began (NBC News). What this means for the people living along the line is described on the page Why North Gaza.
