Cookies & local storage

This translation is provided for information only. The German version is legally binding.

This website does not set any cookies. There is no tracking, no statistics, no advertising and no embedded content from Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or Google. So you don't have to “accept” anything.

What we store in your browser

Just three small entries in your browser's local storage (localStorage) – and only if you use the relevant feature. They never leave your device.

EntryPurposeHow long
dar-zugangRemembers that you have entered the password for this website, which is not yet public. Only a check value is stored, not the password.until you delete it
dar-hinweisRemembers that you have closed the notice about cookies and data protection, so that it doesn't appear on every page.until you delete it
wk-spracheRemembers your language (German, English, Arabic) in the wish calendar.until you delete it

All entries are needed for a feature you use yourself; no consent is required for this (Section 25 (2) No. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG).
