    What your contribution achievesFood sponsor, care sponsor, lifetime sponsor
    • from €10 a month: Food for one cat, with regular news and photos.
    • from €25 a month: Food, care and a share of vaccinations – with personal updates about your sponsor cat.
    • from €50 a month: All-round care including vet and neutering – for one cat or a whole family, for example a mother with kittens.

    The amounts are suggestions – every contribution helps.

    Giving a cat her nameNaming sponsorship and name auction

    Naming sponsorship: As a sponsor from €25 a month, you may give a name to a cat who does not have one yet – for example the tabby mother cat or the black-and-white cat. The name is shown on the page, with you as the sponsor if you wish.

    Name auction (starts with the founding of the gUG): If an animal has no naming sponsor, the highest bid within the auction period earns the right to choose the name. The proceeds go entirely to the care of the animals.

    Reliability and ending a sponsorshipEnd any time, no contract

    You can end your sponsorship at any time. A name that has been given stays for as long as the sponsorship lasts. If contributions stop for two months, we will remind you; if no payment arrives within 30 days after that, the name may be given to someone else.
