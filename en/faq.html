Frequently asked questions

Your question isn't here? Write to us.

Donations

Does my donation really arrive?

We work directly with our team and our partners on the ground. Expenses are approved under the two-person rule and documented with receipts and photos. We show exactly how this works under Transparency.

Who gets my money?

Until the non-profit UG is founded, your support goes directly to the private individuals who run the respective project. Each way to donate states who the recipient is. To the status of the founding

Do I get a donation receipt?

Not yet. As long as the gUG has not been founded and recognised as non-profit by the tax office, your support is a private gift – without a donation receipt and not tax-deductible.

Why are you founding a gUG?

So that the help doesn't depend on individuals but gets a permanent, transparent umbrella – with articles of association, clear rules and, once recognised, donation receipts. Learn more

Can I donate for a specific purpose?

Yes. Put “Animal welfare” or “Children's aid” as the payment reference. We use earmarked donations only for that purpose.

Is it even possible to provide help in Gaza?

Yes, but under severe conditions. Much is hard to get and expensive, and every route can be dangerous. That is why we work with people who live there themselves and know what is possible.

Sponsorship

How does a sponsorship work?

You support an animal or our work with a monthly contribution and receive regular news. You can find all the levels under Sponsorship & naming.

What happens to the name if I stop paying?

If contributions stop for two months, we will remind you. If no payment follows within 30 days after that, the name goes into the next name auction.

Can I end a sponsorship?

Yes, at any time. A short message is enough. The animal of course stays in our care.

Our work

Why animal welfare when so many people are suffering?

For us it is not either/or. Sick and starving animals are also a danger to people, for example through rabies. And compassion doesn't stop at the line between humans and animals. That is why we work hand in hand with humanitarian aid and also support children.

Why North Gaza in particular?

Because along the so-called Yellow Line the need is especially great and the least help arrives. You can find the background with sources under Why North Gaza.

Why don't you show the people on the ground with names and faces?

To protect them. We only publish names, faces or places with consent and only if it endangers no one.

Do you only work in Gaza?

Our work began in Gaza and has its focus there. With culture, inclusion and integration, we are also active in Germany. In the long term, we would like to help elsewhere too, step by step.
